## Dependency Graph

```
M1 (Visual Identity)  ──────────────────────┐
                                             ├──→ M4 (Infrastructure)
M2 (Build & DevEx)    ──→ M3 (Data Layer) ──┘
```

- M3 requires M2#4 (gulp integration) so builds are reliable during refactoring
